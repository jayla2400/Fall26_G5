# Project 2: Study Habit Tracker Prototype

## My Role
My assigned task is Study Session Logging. This feature allows students to record how much time they spend studying for each course.

## Prototype 1: Main Dashboard
The main dashboard shows students their courses, study goals, and completed study hours. Students can select Log Study Session to record their study time.

## Prototype 2: Log Study Session
This screen allows students to choose a course, enter the study date, and record how long they studied. After entering the information, students click Save Session.

## Prototype 3: Study History
The study history screen displays previously saved study sessions. Students can review their courses, study dates, and total study hours.

## How Students Will Use the App
Students will open the Study Habit Tracker and select a course. They will log their study sessions and save the information. The app will help them keep track of their study habits and progress toward their goals.
